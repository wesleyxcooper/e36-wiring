ac-compressor

Diagram

Alibaba PD2-18012AJA 12V electric scroll compressor (18cc, 10,000 BTU, R134a) with included PWM speed controller. Phase 3b installation — the belt-driven OEM A/C compressor is removed at the 07K swap; this electric unit replaces it. No belt drive -- compressor mounts anywhere in engine bay.
Under the H2O engine-bay-mount architecture (see docs/vendor/maxxecu/MaxxECU_RACE_H2O.md): MaxxECU is engine-bay-mounted. Signal chain:
1. Cabin AC switch (dash panel) closes to +12V/GND
2. → OEM E36 X20 pin 4 wire (A/C Compressor Relay, SW/BL) — already an OEM
cabin↔engine crossing, repurposed under the new arch. No new firewall
penetration. Preferred over adding a Maven Connector A cavity.
3. → Engine-bay MaxxECU DIN input — MaxxECU reads AC request for idle-up
compensation and CAN-commands PMU16 to enable the compressor
4. → PMU16 low-current output (~150 mA) drives the 100A relay COIL
5. → 100A relay CONTACT (from battery via appropriate fuse) delivers
switched +12V to the PWM controller +12V input
6. → PWM controller drives the compressor motor at variable duty cycle

Why a discrete 100A relay AND PMU16: the PD2-18012AJA running current (~50-80 A) exceeds PMU16 per-output rating (25 A per output, 50 A even paralleled per Ecumaster PMU_Manual.pdf p.1). So the relay is DISCRETE (not part of PMU16), but PMU16 CONTROLS the relay coil. The 100A relay handles the actual compressor current directly from battery.
Compressor power (100A relay contact) and the PWM controller are entirely engine-bay-side.
Cross-reference schematics/ac-compressor-pwm.py for circuit-level detail and harnesses/power-distribution.wv for PMU16 output allocation. Source: Rawrkee Episode 3 (YouTube) + @jfantis comments.
