ac-compressor

Diagram

Alibaba PD2-18012AJA 12V electric scroll compressor (18cc, 10,000 BTU, R134a) with included PWM speed controller. Controlled via cabin AC switch + 100A relay; MaxxECU RACE DIN receives AC enable signal for idle-up compensation. No belt drive -- compressor mounts anywhere in engine bay. Cross-reference schematics/ac-compressor-pwm.py for circuit-level detail. Source: Rawrkee Episode 3 (YouTube) + @jfantis comments.
