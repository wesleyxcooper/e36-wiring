fuel-pump-hanger-phase1

Diagram

PHASE 1 fuel pump wiring: Radium 20-1170 hanger + Walbro F90000267 (E85-safe, 465 LPH) driven by a DISCRETE aftermarket high-current relay in the engine bay. Relay coil is triggered by the MaxxECU M50 pre-terminated harness's fuel pump signal wire. This is the industry-standard approach for high-current aftermarket fuel pumps — see vendor citation below.
Why a discrete relay in Phase 1: PMU16 is NOT installed until Phase 3 (07K swap moment). Under Phase 1, MaxxECU RACE H2O is engine-bay-mounted but it drives its GPO outputs at low current (~150 mA typical) — it cannot directly carry the pump's ~14-17 A running current. And the OEM K6301 Fuel Pump Relay that this circuit REPLACES is designed for the ~5-10 A OEM in-tank pump, at the upper edge of its rating with a Walbro F90000267. Motorsport convention is a dedicated aftermarket relay + fuse + heavier gauge wire (per JDT Racing Rewire Kit vendor guidance below).
Pump current draw citation:
walbrofuelpumps.com/450lph-walbro-e85-racing-fuel-pump-f90000267.html
"465 lph @ 40 psi and 13.5V (14.1 amps) in gasoline"
(E85 slightly higher due to fuel density — plan for ~15-17 A running current)

Convention citation (aftermarket rewire kit):
jdtracing.com/products/walbro-ti-f90000267-450lph-fuel-pump-w-install-kit-rewire-kit-e85-compatible
"One of the most common mistakes people make when trying to run a high volume
fuel pump is trying to use the factory wiring which on most vehicles is
extremely inadequate. When installing a much larger than factory fuel pump
you need to upgrade the wiring, fuse and relay setup."
The JDT Racing Rewire Kit includes: new relay, 30A inline fuse, 10 AWG wire,
pass-through grommet, ring terminals. Same architecture used here.

Phase 3 transition: at 07K swap moment, this discrete relay is REMOVED and replaced by PMU16 O4 direct-drive. See harnesses/fuel-pump-hanger-phase3.wv. The 12 AWG cabin/tunnel wire from firewall grommet → tunnel → hanger pump(+) stud stays in place — only the engine-bay end changes (relay contact → PMU16 O4). Minimal Phase 3 rewiring.
OEM E36 fuel pump routing (that this wire mirrors): K6301 Fuel Pump Relay pin 87 (engine-bay Front Power Distribution Box) → X20 pin 13 → cabin body harness → trunk/tank → in-tank pump. Source: E36 ETM section 0670.2 via carmanualsonline.info/bmw-318is-1999-e36-electrical-troubleshooting-manual. Under our Phase 1 arch, the discrete added relay REPLACES the K6301 relay function; X20 pin 13 (previously the OEM fuel pump feed) is cavity-plugged.
Cross-reference fuel-pump-hanger-reference.md for pump/hanger specs and MTune config (fuel pump signal function assignment). Install PDF: radiumauto.com/pages/instructions (20-1170).
