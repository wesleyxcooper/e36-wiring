⚠️ PHASE 3 INSTALL FILE. PMU16 is NOT installed in the car during Phase 1 or Phase 2b. Under Phase 1 (M52 + M50 pre-terminated harness), aftermarket loads that PMU16 will eventually drive are handled by:
- Discrete aftermarket high-current relays (e.g., fuel pump — see
harnesses/fuel-pump-hanger-phase1.wv)
- MaxxECU GPO outputs driving small relay coils (e.g., reverse light —
see harnesses/8hp-body-integrations-phase1.wv)
- OEM E36 relays that remain in place until replaced (K6300 DME main,
K21/K22 fan relays, K19 A/C compressor)
Phase 2b is offline harness/engine build — no in-car install work happens. At Phase 3b swap moment PMU16 arrives and replaces all of the above at once.
Ecumaster PMU16 power management unit replaces relay board, fuse block, and DC SSR from earlier design. PMU16 provides 16 programmable MOSFET high-side outputs with per-channel digital fusing, current monitoring, PWM capability, and CAN control from MaxxECU RACE H2O.
MOUNT LOCATION: Engine bay, intake side (RHD passenger side), alongside the MaxxECU RACE H2O in the OEM DME E-box cavity area. IP60 aluminium enclosure (per PMU_Manual.pdf p.1) — shelter under the OEM E-box plastic cover for splash protection. Operating temp AECQ100 Grade 1 (-40 to +125 C) per Ecumaster manual, so engine-bay ambient is within spec. Source: docs/vendor/ecumaster/PMU16_Manual_v101.pdf; details in
docs/vendor/maxxecu/MaxxECU_RACE_H2O.md §8.

IMPLICATION: With both PMU16 and MaxxECU H2O engine-bay-mounted, ECU +12V (PMU16 O1) and 8HP TCU power (KL30/KL15) are engine-bay-to-engine-bay runs. No firewall crossing for ECU or 8HP power lines.
Phase 3b install file. All wire lengths are stubs -- confirm before building. Cross-reference harnesses/maxxecu-07k.wv for ECU CAN pin assignments (Phase 3b ECU harness) and harnesses/8hp-can.wv for 8HP TCU KL30/KL15 destinations.
