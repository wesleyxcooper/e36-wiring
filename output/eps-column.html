eps-column

Diagram

EPowerSteering.com BMW e36 kit (KIT-33). Column-assist EPS -- replaces the mechanical steering column with a motorized unit; the existing hydraulic (or Z3) rack stays in place and functions as a manual rack. Standalone system, no MaxxECU/CAN interface. Cross-reference project plan Power Steering row (rhd e36.md) and Condor Speed Shop RHD steering shaft (below the firewall, unaffected by this column-side conversion).
