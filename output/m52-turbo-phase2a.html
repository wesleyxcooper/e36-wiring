m52-turbo-phase2a

Diagram

Phase 2a delta wiring vs Phase 1 NA M52. Covers boost solenoid (GPO 1 BOOST_SOL stub), reverse light relay reroute from GPO 1 to GPO 8/TACHO (16-pin pin 4), and IAT2 charge air temp sensor (16-pin AIN 2, pin 13). MAP sensor swap is documented as a component note only (no new wiring required). The M50 pre-terminated harness remains in place.
