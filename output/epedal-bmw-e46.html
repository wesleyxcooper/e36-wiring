epedal-bmw-e46

Diagram

BMW E46 accelerator pedal module (35426786282 manual / 35426786281 auto) wired to MaxxECU RACE. Phase 3 (07K engine). MaxxECU is cabin-mounted on the interior firewall face. APS wiring is CABIN-TO-CABIN ONLY -- no firewall bulkhead crossing required. Six wires run from pedal (footwell) to MaxxECU C2 connector and the main CMC sensor supply rail. AS79 bulkhead pins 72-77 are NOT used for APS; they remain spare. Confirmed C2 pin assignments: APS1 → C2 E4 (AIN 6), APS2 → C2 F1 (AIN 7). Source: MaxxECU RACE GEN1 C2 pinout (maxxecu.com/webhelp/wirings-maxxecu_pinout.html). Cross-reference docs/dbw-pinouts.md for full specs and TB pinouts. Pedal PN is manual vs auto -- NOT LHD vs RHD. Same part in any market.
